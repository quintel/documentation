---
title: Non-road mobile machinery
---
Non-road mobile machinery (NRMM) covers vehicles and equipment with a combustion engine that are not used on public roads, such as tractors and harvesters in agriculture or cranes and loaders in construction. Energy statistics report the fuel use of mobile machinery in the sector where the machinery is used.

For almost all regions in the ETM, energy use of mobile machinery is part of the sectors where the activity takes place, which is in line with energy statistics. For the Dutch national and regional datasets, however, this energy use is allocated to road transport. The table below shows how this is done for the relevant datasets.

The reallocation of mobile machinery fuel use increases the energy demand of road transport in the start year. This also affects the technology split (modal split) shown in the start year of a scenario: this can therefore differ from modal splits reported in national statistics.

| Dataset | Start year | Allocation of mobile machinery |
|---|---|---|
| Netherlands | 2023 | Fuel use allocated to all road vehicles |
| Netherlands | 2019 | Diesel and biodiesel use allocated to trucks, LPG to road vehicles using LPG |
| Dutch provinces, (RES) regions and municipalities | 2023 | Fuel use allocated to all road vehicles |
