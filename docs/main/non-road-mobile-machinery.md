---
title: Non-road mobile machinery
---
Non-road mobile machinery (NRMM) covers vehicles and equipment with a combustion engine that are not used on public roads, such as tractors and harvesters in agriculture or cranes and loaders in construction. Energy statistics report the fuel use of mobile machinery in the sector where the machinery is used, for example the Agriculture sector.

For almost all regions in the ETM, energy use of mobile machinery is part of the sectors where the activity takes place, which is in line with energy statistics. For the Dutch national and regional datasets, however, this energy use is allocated to road transport. The table below shows how this is done for the relevant datasets.

| Dataset | Start year | Allocation of mobile machinery |
|---|---|---|
| Netherlands (national) | 2023 | Diesel and biodiesel use of NRMM distributed proportionally over all road vehicles using diesel (cars, vans, trucks and buses), LPG use distributed proportionally over all road vehicles using LPG (cars and vans) |
| Netherlands (provinces, RES regions and municipalities) | 2023 | Fossil fuel and biofuel use of NRMM counted as respectively diesel and biodiesel, distributed proportionally over all road vehicles using diesel (cars, vans, trucks and buses) |
| Netherlands | 2019 | Diesel and biodiesel use of NRMM allocated to trucks, LPG use distributed proportionally over all road vehicles using LPG (cars and vans) |

:::warning Modal and technology split
The reallocation of mobile machinery also affects the modal and technology split shown in the start year of a scenario: this can therefore differ from reported national statistics.
:::
