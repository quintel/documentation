#### Simplified heat sliders for buildings and households

* The way the building and household stock is configured has been simplified: considerably fewer
sliders are now needed to set the housing and building stock and their heat demands. The same building
and household categories are still used. This is available in the <a href="https://energytransitionmodel.com/scenario/demand/households/population-and-housing-stock" target="_blank">Population & housing stock</a> and the <a href="https://energytransitionmodel.com/scenario/demand/buildings/building-stock" target="_blank">Building stock</a>
* Heat demand for buildings and households is no longer set directly via sliders in kWh/m2.
Instead, separate insulation and behaviour sliders determine heat demand, making it more intuitive to configure.

#### Single sign-on across the Energy Transition Model

* Signing in now works across the whole Energy Transition Model domain.
Switching between applications (ETModel, ETSource, ETEngine, and others) no longer requires signing in again for each one.

#### IPCC-based direct emissions reporting

* Direct emissions can now be read out per IPCC reporting category,
in addition to the existing breakdown per ETM sector. This new breakdown is available in the chart overview.

#### New emissions inputs

* New sliders have been added in ETEngine (not available in ETModel) that allow users to adjust non-CO2 and non-energetic emissions.
