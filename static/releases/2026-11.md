#### New features

* The <a href="https://collections.energytransitionmodel.com" target="_blank">Collections</a> interface has been redesigned, with easier navigation between inputs and outputs. New charts show final energy consumption by use type, sector, subsector and carrier, including a chart in line with the EU target. Results can now also be shown in tonnes of oil equivalent (toe).

* A new chart shows direct emissions per Dutch climate sector (klimaatsector), the classification from the 2019 Climate Agreement (Klimaatakkoord) that is common in the Netherlands. With 1990 as the reference year, scenarios can be checked against national reduction targets. More information can be found on the <a href="../../main/direct-emissions" target="_blank">direct emissions method</a> page.

* A new dataset for the Island of Ireland (2019) has been added. It combines the Republic of Ireland and Northern Ireland into a single dataset.

* The liquid fuels production in the ETM can be coupled to an external model that has now been extended. Coupled to this external model, the methanol yield through gasification can be adjusted by setting an additional input of hydrogen. Furthermore, the CO2 utilisation can be set for Fischer-Tropsch and methanol synthesis. Finally, the CO2 capture in the transformation processes of the chemical industry can now also be set.
