### New features

#### Heat in the built environment
* The technical and financial properties of
<a href="https://energytransitionmodel.com/scenario/demand/households/space-heating-and-hot-water"
target="_blank">air heat pumps and hybrid heat pumps</a> for households have been updated. This
includes the COP of these heat pumps, which results in a
more realistic share of residences heated with these technologies in the start year.
* A new chart "Residential space heating demand per residence" is available, which shows the
space heating demand per residence per construction period for apartments, detached houses,
semi-detached houses and terraced houses. Find this chart when making assumptions for
<a href="https://energytransitionmodel.com/scenario/demand/households/insulation"
target="_blank">insulation</a> or in the charts overview.

<img src="/img/releases/residential_heat_demand_per_residence_chart.png" width="650" />

#### Other improvements
* The efficiency of electric cars, motorcycles and buses, expressed in pkm/MJ,
was outdated and has been updated. Passenger transport technologies can be set in the
<a href="https://energytransitionmodel.com/scenario/demand/transport_passenger_transport/applications"
target="_blank">Transport</a> section.


### Bug fixes

* For some regions,
<a href="https://energytransitionmodel.com/scenario/demand/households/population-and-housing-stock"
target="_blank">newly built residences</a> had a higher space heating demand per residence than
residences from older construction periods. This has been corrected: new residences now have the
same heat demand per residence as the most recent existing construction period for that residence
type. Scenario results may have changed due to this bug fix.
