#### New features

* Air conditioning has been added as a space heating technology for households, in the <a href="https://energytransitionmodel.com/scenario/demand/households/space-heating-and-hot-water" target="_blank">Space heating & hot water</a> section. Two variants are available: air conditioning combined with a heat pump boiler for hot water, and a hybrid air conditioning combined with a gas boiler. More information can be found on the <a href="../../main/heat-pumps" target="_blank">Heat pumps</a> page.

* Refinery gas from oil refineries is now used to meet the heat demand of refineries and the other chemical industry. The distribution between these two sectors can be set in the <a href="https://energytransitionmodel.com/scenario/supply/fuel_production/oil-refineries" target="_blank">Oil refineries</a> section and the remaining heat demand is met by other heat technologies. Refinery gas is also included in the "Liquid fuels and feedstock flows" and "Final demand of oil per sector and per product" charts. More information can be found on the <a href="../../main/liquid-fuels" target="_blank">Liquid fuels and feedstock</a> page.

* A new <a href="https://energytransitionmodel.com/scenario/emissions/other_emissions_direct/overview" target="_blank">Direct emissions</a> section has been added under Greenhouse gases, where the static direct emissions per sector can be set. More information can be found on the <a href="../../main/direct-emissions" target="_blank">Direct emissions</a> page.

* When the chemical industry is coupled to an external model, the captured CO2 is now allocated to the fertilizers, refineries and other chemical industry sectors. This allocation is shown in the "Captured CO2 flows" and "Demand and supply of captured CO2 (CCUS)" charts in the <a href="https://energytransitionmodel.com/scenario/emissions/ccus/capture-of-co2-in-industry-sector" target="_blank">Capture of CO2 in industry sector</a> section.

* New queries for the peak of flexible and inflexible electricity demand have been added. Since these two peaks can occur in different hours, their sum can differ from the total electricity demand peak.

* A new dataset for Kop van de Veluwe (2023) has been added. It combines the municipalities of Elburg, Hattem, Heerde, Nunspeet and Oldebroek into a single dataset.
