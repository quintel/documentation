### New features

#### Direct emissions
* A new section has been added to set <a href="https://energytransitionmodel.com/scenario/emissions/other_emissions_direct/overview" target="_blank">other greenhouse gas emissions</a> of a scenario. These sliders only have effect on **direct emissions** results. Read more about the direct emissions method in the <a href="../../main/direct-emissions" target="_blank">documentation</a>.
* Like the national datasets, the Dutch regional datasets now also contain the other greenhouse gas emissions for the direct emissions method in the start year. This was previously not the case.

#### Other new features
* Air conditioning has been added as a <a href="https://energytransitionmodel.com/scenario/demand/households/space-heating-and-hot-water" target="_blank">space heating technology</a> for households. Two variants are available: air conditioning combined with a heat pump boiler for hot water and hybrid air conditioning combined with a gas-fired boiler. Read the <a href="../../main/heat-pumps" target="_blank">documentation</a> for more information.
* Refinery gas produced by oil refineries can now be used to meet the heat demand of refineries and the chemical sector. The distribution of refinery gas used in these two sectors can be set in the <a href="https://energytransitionmodel.com/scenario/supply/fuel_production/oil-refineries" target="_blank">Oil refineries</a> section. Read the <a href="../../main/liquid-fuels#refinery-gas" target="_blank">documentation</a> for more information on how refinery gas is modelled.
* New Dutch regional datasets have been added, which are composed of a combination of underlying municipalities. The new datasets are: Kop van de Veluwe, Zeeuws-Vlaanderen, Schouwen-Duiveland en Tholen, Walcheren and Bevelanden. Go to the <a href="https://data.energytransitionmodel.com/" target="_blank">Dataset Manager</a> to view these datasets.

### Bug fixes

* Road fuel demand in the Dutch regional datasets was overestimated because fuel demand of trucks and two-wheelers was counted twice. This has now been corrected.
