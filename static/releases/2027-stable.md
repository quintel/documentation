## Stable version release 2027
On 10 December we will release a new stable version of the Energy Transition Model: `#2027-stable`.

What this means for you:
- **Downtime on 10 december**: The ETM will be offline from the morning of December 10th.
- **Retired datasets**: Five outdated datasets will be retired from `#latest` (see the list below). Saved scenarios that use one of these datasets will be moved to `#2027-stable` automatically.
- **Nothing changes**: for all other scenarios in `#latest` or in the current stable version, `#2025-01`.

### Retired datasets
After the release, these datasets will only be available in `#2027-stable`:

- Netherlands (2015) - `nl`
- European Union (2011) - `eu`
- Singapore - `SG_singapore`
- Oostende - `BEGM35013_oostende`
- Turnhout - `BEGM13040_turnhout`

_Working on one of these datasets?_ Save your scenario to My Scenarios before 10 December. Scenarios that are not saved to your account will be lost.

### Downtime on December 10th
The ETM is taken offline in the morning and brought back online as soon as possible. During this time we move the scenarios mentioned above to `#2027-stable`. Taking the model offline makes sure the moved scenarios are exactly the same as the originals.
